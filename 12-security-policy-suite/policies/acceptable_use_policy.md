# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Document owner:** CISO | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-08-01
**Review cycle:** Annual | **Classification:** Internal
**ISO 27001:2022 references:** A.5.10, A.6.3, A.8.1, A.8.7

## 1. Purpose

To define the acceptable use of organizational information systems,
devices, and data, protecting both the organization and its users from
harm caused by misuse.

## 2. Scope

All users of organizational systems and all devices — corporate or
personal (BYOD) — used to access organizational information.

## 3. Acceptable Use

- Systems and data are provided for legitimate business purposes;
  limited, reasonable personal use is permitted where it does not
  interfere with duties or violate this policy.
- Users protect their credentials, lock unattended sessions, and report
  suspected security incidents promptly.
- Data is handled per its classification (see *Data Classification &
  Handling Policy*).

## 4. Prohibited Use

Users must **not**:
- Share credentials or attempt to access data/systems beyond their
  authorization.
- Install unapproved software or disable security controls (endpoint
  protection, disk encryption, patching).
- Transmit Confidential or Restricted data over unapproved channels
  (personal email, unsanctioned cloud storage, unencrypted media).
- Use organizational systems for illegal activity, harassment, or to
  knowingly introduce malware.
- Connect unapproved devices to production or restricted network
  segments.

## 5. Email, Messaging, and Internet

- Be alert to phishing; do not click suspicious links or open unexpected
  attachments — report them via the *Incident Response* channel.
- Corporate communications are subject to monitoring and retention
  consistent with law and the organization's privacy notice.

## 6. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices accessing organizational data must enroll in mobile
device management (or equivalent), enforce screen lock and encryption,
and permit remote wipe of organizational data on loss or offboarding.

## 7. Acknowledgement

Users acknowledge this policy at onboarding and annually thereafter as
part of security awareness training.

## 8. Compliance

Violations are handled under the Information Security Policy and may
result in disciplinary action.
