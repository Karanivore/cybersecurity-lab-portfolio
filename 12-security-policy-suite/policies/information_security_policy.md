# Information Security Policy

**Document owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
**Approved by:** Executive Leadership / Board
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-08-01 | **Review cycle:** Annual
**Classification:** Internal

> Template / illustrative policy for a portfolio. Tailor scope, roles, and
> obligations to the adopting organization before use.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes management's direction and commitment to
protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA) of
information assets. It is the apex document of the organization's
Information Security Management System (ISMS) and sets the framework
under which all subordinate security policies operate.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and third
parties who access organizational information or systems, and to all
information assets regardless of format (electronic, physical, verbal)
or location (on-premises, cloud, remote).

## 3. Policy Statements

1. **Risk-based approach.** Security controls shall be selected and
   prioritized based on documented risk assessments (see the Cyber Risk
   Assessment process) aligned to the organization's risk appetite.
2. **Framework alignment.** The ISMS is aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and
   the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.
3. **Least privilege.** Access to information and systems is granted on a
   need-to-know, least-privilege basis (see *Access Control Policy*).
4. **Data protection.** Information is classified and handled according
   to its sensitivity (see *Data Classification & Handling Policy*).
5. **Incident response.** Security events are reported and managed under
   the *Incident Response Policy*.
6. **Acceptable use.** All users abide by the *Acceptable Use Policy*.
7. **Continuous improvement.** The ISMS is monitored, measured, and
   improved; management reviews security performance at least annually.
8. **Legal and regulatory compliance.** The organization meets applicable
   legal, regulatory, and contractual security obligations.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board / Executive Leadership | Approve the ISMS, set risk appetite, provide resources |
| CISO | Own the ISMS, maintain policies, report on security posture |
| Information/System Owners | Classify assets, define access requirements |
| IT Operations | Implement and operate technical controls |
| All personnel | Comply with policies, complete training, report incidents |

## 5. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations of this policy or its subordinate policies may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination, and where
applicable, civil or criminal liability. Exceptions require documented
risk acceptance approved by the CISO.

## 6. Related Documents

Access Control Policy · Acceptable Use Policy · Incident Response Policy ·
Data Classification & Handling Policy · Risk Assessment procedure.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after any significant
change to the business, technology, threat, or regulatory environment.
